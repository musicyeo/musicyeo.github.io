🌿 About Me

I’m Faye, and this is where my love for a calm, well-ordered home comes to life.

By day, I work as a Quality Assurance Manager in the IT domain, which is a fancy way of saying I have a natural eye for the tiniest details. At home, that professional eye transforms into a deep passion for minimalism and home organization. I absolutely love creating intentional, clutter-free spaces, and I am incredibly meticulous about every single item we bring into our household—especially when curating thoughtful gift ideas and practical essentials for our family and friends. When I am not organizing, you can find me lost in a good book, experimenting with new recipes in the kitchen, interacting with my pet fishes, or finding creative ways to play with and educate my beautiful kids. I also have a deep appreciation for smart technology and how it can make a modern home run more smoothly.

My husband is the perfect energetic counterweight to my quiet love for indoor order. He is a huge fan of outdoor activities, hitting the gym, and filling our home with music—whether he is singing or playing the guitar, drums, or keyboard. True to his technical expertise in Image Processing, Computer Graphics, Computer Vision, and Pattern Classification, he thinks deeply about everything, almost always analyzing the world around him, even the most trivial daily details!

LivingWithFaye is a space born out of our shared life. It is a genuine place where we want to share the good, simple things that bring us joy and peace. But to be completely honest and transparent with you, there is a practical side to this space too. With the ever-rising costs of daily life, we hope to earn a little extra income through affiliate commissions when you purchase items we genuinely love and recommend.

Thank you for stepping into our world, and we hope our reflections bring a little more ease, utility, and comfort to your daily routines.
